# Model card: lymph-node metastasis patch classifier

Generated by `python -m pcam.model_card` from `reports/metrics.json`, `reports/onnx-test.json` and the selection runs in `reports/`.

## Model

- EfficientNetV2-B0 (ImageNet weights) fine-tuned, one logit, mixed precision; trained 2026-10-18 on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060, 8188 MiB (WSL2), 5 epochs in 8.7 minutes.
- Input: one 96x96 RGB patch, raw 0-255 values. Output: one logit; probability = sigmoid(logit / 1.528) (temperature fitted on the validation split).
- Served as ONNX (opset 17, 23.5 MB) by the Rust service. Largest difference from TensorFlow on 2,048 test patches: 6.50e-03 in probability.

## Intended use

Research and demonstration: highlight regions of lymph-node sections that may contain metastatic tissue so a pathologist looks there first. **Not a medical device and not validated for diagnosis.**

### The exported model the service runs

All 32,768 test patches through the float32 ONNX export (onnxruntime CPU, 20 threads, batches of 256): **AUC 0.9410** (0.9387-0.9436) against 0.9409 for TensorFlow. Probabilities differ by 0.00034 on average and at most 0.0121 (float16 training against a float32 export); 13 of 32,768 decisions at 0.5 change. Scored at 1,400 patches/s.

Slide level (129 test slides, 49 with tumour; slide score = its highest patch probability): AUC 0.975 (95% CI 0.946-0.995). Only patches PCam sampled are scored, not the whole slide.

## Model selection (validation split only)

Each learning rate was trained with early stopping on validation AUC and scored on the validation split; the test split was read once, by the final run of the chosen one.

| Learning rate | Best epoch | Validation AUC | Training time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.001 | 1 of 4 | 0.9625 | 6.3 min |
| 0.0003 | 2 of 5 | 0.9584 | 7.7 min |
| 0.0001 | 2 of 5 | 0.9578 | 8.8 min |

The final run of the chosen rate stopped after 5 epochs at validation AUC 0.9629. GPU kernels are not bit-for-bit deterministic, so a rerun with the same seed can peak at a different epoch; early stopping chose the epoch on validation either way.

## Limitations

- PCam patches come from one challenge (two Dutch medical centres, Camelyon16); other scanners, stains and tissues will shift the inputs. Stain augmentation reduces but does not remove this.
- The label is tumour in the centre 32x32 pixels; patches with tumour only at the edges are labelled negative.
- PCam's original splits contain duplicate patches from probabilistic sampling; results are comparable with the PCam paper, which used the same splits.
- The served model runs a single pass; test-time augmentation is measured above but costs 8x the compute.
